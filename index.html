How it works

No new app. It all happens on the WhatsApp you already use.

  1. Tell it the due

    Send a voice note or text on WhatsApp, in Hindi, Punjabi or Hinglish.

  2. Check the draft

    Hisaab Saathi writes a warm, respectful reminder with a UPI link and a part-payment option.

  3. Approve and send

    Edit it or send it as is. Nothing goes out without your okay.

Built for the way your shop works

Your language, your voice

Speak or type the due the way you normally would. Hindi, Punjabi and Hinglish all work.

Polite, neighbourly wording with a UPI link. Customers can pay part now and the rest later.

You stay in control

Every message waits for your approval. You decide what goes, and when.

Questions shop owners ask

Will my customers feel insulted?

No. Reminders are written to be warm and respectful, like you'd speak to a neighbour. You read every message before it goes, and you can change any word.

Do I need to download a new app?

No. Everything works on WhatsApp, which you already use every day.

Which languages does it support?

Hindi, Punjabi and Hinglish, by voice note or text.

What happens with my data?

Your entries are used only to draft your reminders. The Credit Passport is optional and is shared with a lender only if you give consent.
